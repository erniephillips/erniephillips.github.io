CAD and Fusion 360 workflow

Professional tooling for legitimate design-to-print work.

EPATA LLC benefits from CAD and web software that supports parametric modeling, dimensional control, model intake, AI-assisted review, clean exports, and repeatable revisions. That matters when a part needs to fit, survive use, be quoted clearly, and be made again later.

Parametric modeling Assemblies Manufacturing exports AI-assisted intake
  1. Measure

    Photos, dimensions, constraints, failure points, and the job the part needs to do.

  2. Model

    CAD geometry built for fit, print orientation, tolerances, and material limits.

  3. Prototype

    Print, inspect, test, and revise until the part performs the intended work.

  4. Deliver

    Finished parts, source files when appropriate, and practical notes for the next run.
