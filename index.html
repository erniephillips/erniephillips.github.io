02 / Project fit

Broad capability. Direct accountability.

Physical, digital, and hybrid projects all start with a defined problem and a practical review. You work directly with me from the first message through delivery.

Production runs and rush work may be available when the part, quantity, and current schedule line up.

+ Usually a good fit

  • A functional part, replacement component, prototype, repeat order, or production run
  • Dimensions, samples, drawings, files, or an existing system I can evaluate
  • A website, application, automation, AI workflow, or hybrid project with a clear job to do

− Usually not a fit

  • Regulated, certified, medical, aerospace, or safety-critical engineering
  • Original characters, faces, creatures, or free-form organic sculpture
  • An undefined “build everything” request without a first priority

03 / Process

Review first.
Then commit.

Your first message starts a fit and feasibility review. It does not obligate either of us or imply that the project has already been accepted.

  1. 01

    Show me the problem

    Send the goal, what you already have, important dimensions or integrations, quantity, intended use, deadline, and budget range. Photos, sketches, files, and links all help.

  2. 02

    Define the realistic scope

    I review feasibility, risks, unknowns, schedule, and likely approach. If it is a fit, you receive a clear scope and price or estimate before work begins.

  3. 03

    Build, test, and hand off

    I complete the agreed physical or digital work, test what the scope calls for, and deliver the part, files, software, documentation, or next-step recommendations.
