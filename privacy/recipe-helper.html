Recipe Helper privacy policy

EPATA LLC · com.epata.homeflow · Updated November 18, 2026

Your kitchen information

Recipe Helper keeps recipes, ingredients, pantry and stock records, shopping prices, cooking instructions, progress, and saved food avoidances on your device. Entering an allergy or diet preference does not send it to EPATA, advertising, sign-in or purchase-verification services. Recipe matching uses the cookbook and saved names on the device. Core tools require no account, payment or advertisement.

Photos, text recognition and speech

You choose photos through Android's camera or photo picker. Bundled Google ML Kit reads selected images on the device. Recipe Helper does not send images or recognized recipe text to cloud AI. The app cleans up temporary processing files; originals can remain with your camera or storage provider. Google's ML Kit may send technical device, app and SDK performance information for diagnostics and usage measurement. This reporting is separate from your image and recognized text.

Spoken directions use an installed voice that supports offline speech. Recipe Helper does not enable microphone listening or silently switch to a network voice. The speech provider has its own settings and privacy information.

Advertising and privacy choices

The regular app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google advertising services may collect and share IP address, device/account identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. An IP address can indicate an approximate location. Recipe Helper requests no precise location permission and does not add ingredients, recipe searches, images, food avoidances, prices or purchase tokens as advertising data. Requests use the non-personalized setting; this does not eliminate the SDK's technical data processing.

Where applicable, Google's consent forms let you review choices. Return to Settings → Ad privacy choices to change available options. Ads wait for SDK permission. The app pauses requests during privacy forms and invalidates stored creatives when permission changes. Core tools remain available if you decline, are offline or no advertisement is available. Verified ad-free access and earned temporary ad breaks suppress ads. Changing choices does not delete information Google already received. Development builds use Google test ads.

Optional accounts and payments

Where paid features are enabled, Google Play handles payment information; Recipe Helper does not receive payment-card details. Optional Google sign-in and verification use an identity token, device-generated public key and signed proof, product identifiers, purchase tokens and verification status. Household records are excluded. The verification design stores an account identifier derived from the Google account ID and protected purchase records, without storing your Google display name, email address or avatar. Google's account and transaction records follow its policies.

Your controls and retention

Household records remain until you delete or replace them or uninstall the app. Settings → Delete all local household data removes the kitchen workspace. Automatic Android backup and device transfer are disabled. Export local backup creates a file only when you choose; your provider may sync or share it. Deleting the workspace does not delete exports or originals in other apps.

When purchase accounts are available, Plus offers Sign out of Plus and Delete Plus account data. Deletion removes stored purchase associations from the verification service. Google Play purchases, subscriptions and records remain separate; cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Verification records support restoring access and remain until account deletion. Provider data follows the provider's retention rules. Verification requests use HTTPS. Household files use Android app-private storage; these safeguards cannot guarantee protection on a compromised device.

Account deletion requests

To delete purchase-verification records in the app, open Plus while signed in, choose Delete Plus account data, and confirm Delete account data. If you cannot use the app, email android-support@epatallc.com with the subject Recipe Helper account deletion and identify the Google account you used for Plus. We may need to verify that the request belongs to you. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or household backups. Deletion removes the verification service's purchase associations; restoring purchases can recreate them. It does not cancel Google Play subscriptions or delete Google's transaction records. Cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Kitchen records stay on your device until you use Delete all local household data or uninstall.

Support and changes

Contact android-support@epatallc.com for privacy questions or account-data requests. EPATA receives what you choose to send support; avoid including recipes, health details or backups unless necessary. If you believe a child's information was provided to us, contact the same address. We do not create child profiles or ask for a child's identity. Changes in SDKs or data handling can require an updated policy.
