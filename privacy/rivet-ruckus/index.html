Rivet Ruckus privacy policy

Updated November 19, 2026

This policy covers the regular Rivet Ruckus Android game, package com.epata.rivetruckus, provided by EPATA LLC.

Your game progress

Progress, scores, earned cosmetic bolts, selected finishes and preferences are stored on your device. The game has no required account, EPATA cloud save or EPATA game-progress server. Its levels, artwork, font and sound are bundled for offline play. Earned bolts have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. Purchases and subscriptions are currently unavailable.

Advertising and privacy choices

The regular game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banners appear in menus, optional rewarded videos can earn cosmetic bolts, and limited full-screen ads can appear between completed circuits. Banners are hidden during gameplay, Help and other dialogs. Advertising requires a network connection; the game can be played without one.

The app requests an update of privacy information from Google each session. It presents a required consent message in a menu, and requests ads only when UMP indicates that requests are permitted. This permission can include limited or non-personalized advertising under the applicable settings. Declining personalized advertising is not the same as disabling every type of advertising.

Open Settings, then Privacy and ads. When Google makes an ad choices form available, Manage ad choices opens it so you can review or change your choices. If privacy information or a message is unavailable, advertising can remain unavailable while you continue playing. Android's advertising-ID settings also provide device-level controls.

Information used by Google's services

Google's advertising services may receive your IP address, which can indicate general location; advertising or app-set identifiers and other applicable device/account identifiers; interactions such as app launches, taps and video views; and diagnostic information about performance, hangs or errors. These services use information for advertising, measurement/analytics and fraud prevention. UMP processes and stores information needed to determine and remember applicable privacy choices. Data handling depends on the consent choices, region and SDK settings.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. It does not upload your saved game to EPATA. Its use of advertising services means it should not be treated as an app that collects or shares no data.

Service providers and links

Google processes advertising and privacy-choice information under its applicable policies. See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information for advertising and Google My Ad Center. An ad you choose to open may lead to an advertiser's website or service, which has its own privacy practices. EPATA does not operate those services.

Retention and removal

Your local game data remains until it is removed through Android app storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Those actions can permanently remove progress because the game does not provide an EPATA cloud backup. Google retains its service data under its own policies; EPATA does not control a separate copy of that advertising dataset. Use Google's privacy controls and applicable request processes for information Google holds.

Your requests

Privacy rights vary by location and may include access, correction, deletion, restriction or choices about advertising-related sharing. Contact EPATA with questions about the game and your applicable rights. We may need information sufficient to understand a request and will explain when a request concerns data controlled by a service provider. Do not email passwords, government identification or payment details.

Children and families

The game does not ask players to enter a name, date of birth or contact details. A parent or guardian with concerns about a child's use of the game or data processing can contact EPATA. Google advertising and privacy controls remain subject to the app's audience settings and applicable requirements.

Security and updates

The game restricts its embedded game page to bundled content and uses encrypted network transport for Google services. No system can guarantee perfect security. This policy may be updated when the app's actual practices change; the updated date identifies the current text.
