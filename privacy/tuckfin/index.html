Tuckfin Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026

Tuckfin is a family-friendly game intended for children, teenagers and adults. Game progress stays on the device. Google's advertising and consent services may process information as described below.

Current status: Tuckfin's public Google Play release is still being prepared. This interim notice describes the regular Android preview, version 0.2.3. That preview does not yet include age screening or separate advertising handling for children and users of unknown age. Those public-release requirements remain unfinished.

This policy applies to the regular Tuckfin Android application, package com.epata.tuckfin, developed by EPATA LLC.

Your game and local storage

You do not need a Tuckfin account. Game progress, sea glass, owned colors, comfort settings, daily records and an unfinished voyage are stored locally in the app. This version does not send gameplay events or saved voyages to an EPATA server and does not include an EPATA analytics service.

The game does not request your contacts, microphone, photos, documents or precise location. Required game art, fonts and sound are included in the installation. Gameplay works offline.

Advertising and Google services

The current preview includes menu banners, occasional ads between completed voyages, and videos you choose to watch for cosmetic sea glass. Ads do not buy retries or control access to voyages.

Google's advertising and consent SDKs may contact Google and process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • app, device and advertising identifiers, subject to device settings and permissions;
  • ad views, interactions and diagnostic or performance information;
  • privacy choices and information needed to determine which privacy message applies.

Google uses this information to provide advertising, measure performance, prevent fraud and apply privacy choices. Google controls its own retention and processing. Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising.

EPATA does not operate a server that receives your saved game, an EPATA advertising profile or a paid currency ledger in this version. Advertising partners' processing is separate from local game storage.

Your ad privacy choices

The preview checks Google's consent information at launch and uses the result to control its ad requests. The consent SDK itself may contact Google to determine and present the applicable message. This consent check is not an age screen: version 0.2.3 does not distinguish adult, child or unknown-age users when making that check.

When Google's SDK indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, the preview includes Ad privacy choices in Sound & comfort and Privacy & credits. Form availability depends on Google's SDK and publisher configuration. Changing choices discards cached ads before further requests are permitted. Restricting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising.

Declining a privacy choice, an unavailable consent message or an offline connection does not prevent you from playing the game. You can also manage advertising identifiers through your Android privacy settings.

Rewards and purchases

A rewarded video is optional. Its reward is granted locally after Google's earned-reward callback; an unavailable or unsuccessful video grants nothing. Sea glass is a cosmetic game resource, not cash or a paid wallet.

No purchase or subscription checkout is enabled in preview version 0.2.3. This describes the current preview. The app does not collect payment-card or billing-account information. Any purchase processing and related privacy terms will be disclosed before checkout is enabled.

Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Local game information remains on your device until it is removed, for example by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Removing storage deletes your progress; EPATA has no server copy to restore. Installing a normal app update is designed to preserve your existing local progress.

For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy controls and policies linked above. You may contact EPATA about applicable access, deletion or other privacy rights. Because this version has no game accounts or server copies of saved games, we may not hold the local information you ask about.

Intended audience

Tuckfin is intended for children, teenagers and adults. The current preview does not ask players to enter a name, email address, date of birth or age group. The family-audience advertising and data-handling changes for the public release are still being prepared; this notice does not claim those protections are already implemented in version 0.2.3. Contact EPATA with questions about the app's information practices.

Contact and changes

For Tuckfin privacy questions, contact EPATA LLC at android-support@epatallc.com.

We will update this page when the app's information practices change and revise the date above. A new purpose requiring a privacy choice will be handled before that processing begins.
