Wispwake privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Version covered: 0.1.3

This policy covers the regular Wispwake Android game, package com.erniephillips.wispwake, operated by EPATA LLC. The private Family Edition is a separate app and does not include advertising or billing SDKs.

Your local game save and settings

Wispwake stores puzzle progress, settings, unlocked colors, local embers and pending video-reward receipts in its app storage on your device. It does not create or require a Wispwake account or upload this saved game to EPATA. This version is configured to exclude its app data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this local information; keep this consequence in mind before doing so.

The game and its guide work offline. An unavailable advertisement or consent service does not prevent ordinary play or free retries. Wispwake does not ask for your contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone. The guide is a prerecorded video played by the app; Wispwake does not record your voice or access the microphone.

Advertising and Google's services

The regular app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its advertising partners may process information for advertising, measurement and analytics, fraud prevention and service security. Depending on applicable settings and permissions, this can include:

  • Device or advertising identifiers and other service identifiers.
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location.
  • Interactions with advertisements and product interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as crashes, launch times and other technical measurements.

These SDK communications are separate from the locally stored puzzle save. EPATA does not add a separate game-analytics SDK, social sign-in, chat or document-upload service to this version. See Google's privacy policy, Google's information about partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure information.

Ad choices and rewarded videos

For customer advertising, the app requests Google's consent information at launch and presents Google's consent interface when required. Ad requests proceed only when Google's SDK reports that requests are permitted. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the app displays Settings → Privacy → Change ad privacy choices. This button lets you revisit those choices and is not shown when the SDK reports that it is not required. Android and your Google account also provide advertising controls; see My Ad Center.

Rewarded videos are optional. The offer states the local ember reward before a video opens. A reward is recorded only when the native advertising SDK reports that it was earned. Pending earned rewards can be recovered if the app reloads before the game saves them. These credits have no cash value, are not transferable and do not grant purchases, subscriptions or any server-recognized paid balance. Declining a video does not stop ordinary play.

Banners are confined to eligible menu screens. Forced full-screen advertisements are separated from active puzzles and the guide, with opening lessons protected and a minimum interval between eligible appearances. Development and designated test-device ads do not produce publisher revenue.

Purchases and existing ownership

Purchases and subscriptions are not active in this version. Prices shown in the game are inactive purchase previews; selecting them does not start a payment or subscription. The app includes Google Play Billing and, when its Play connection is available, checks for existing purchases and subscriptions for this package. Its native integration can also request store/catalog information. A reported purchased ad-removal item or subscription pauses advertising while ownership verification is needed; this does not establish a verified paid entitlement or a payment-backed balance. This version does not send purchase receipts to an EPATA purchase-verification service. Google handles its own Play account and payment information under its policies.

Retention, deletion and support

Local game information remains until you remove it through Android app-storage controls or uninstall the app. Wispwake has no EPATA-hosted game account to delete. Google's SDK/service information is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices, rather than a retention period controlled by this local game. You can use Google's privacy and account controls for information held by Google.

If you email support, EPATA receives the message and any information you choose to include. Support correspondence is used to respond to the request and is retained as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information or sensitive identity documents.

Children and privacy questions

Wispwake does not ask players to create a profile or provide a name, age or date of birth. Parents and guardians may contact EPATA with privacy questions or concerns about information a child may have provided. Google's advertising services and their privacy choices are described above.

Changes and contact

Updates to the app or its services may require a revised policy. The date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, support or a request concerning information you sent directly to EPATA, email android@epatallc.com.
