Delete purchase-account data

No account is needed to play Waddlebound. Your game save is local to your device. Purchases and the purchase-account service are currently disabled in the version being prepared for Google Play.

Contact us about purchase data

There is currently no active Waddlebound purchase-account service from which to delete records. If you have a privacy question or previously supplied purchase data to EPATA LLC, email android-support@epatallc.com with the app name and your request. Do not send passwords, card details, Google ID tokens or purchase tokens. Ownership may need to be authenticated before a request can be completed.

Before purchase accounts become available, we will update these instructions with the in-app deletion route, data removed, any data retained, and retention periods.

Local game progress

Android's app-data removal and uninstall controls remove local game saves. EPATA LLC has no server copy of your gameplay to restore. Deleting game progress does not cancel a Google Play subscription or refund a purchase. If a subscription exists in your Google Play account, manage its renewal in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
