Waddlebound privacy policy

Your penguin adventure works without an account. This page explains local saves, advertising and choices in the customer Android edition.

Your game stays on your device

Waddlebound stores your completed voyages, medals, shells, cosmetic trails, settings and unfinished adventure on your device. EPATA LLC does not operate a gameplay analytics service or upload your game save. Gameplay, illustrations, the field guide, fonts and synthesized audio are bundled in the app; no account or additional gameplay download is required.

Advertising and age eligibility

Advertising stays off until the player selects an age group. Players under 18, or unsure of their age group, play without advertising. The app stores only this age-group choice on the device, without a date of birth. Choosing “18 or older” establishes eligibility; it does not constitute consent to personalized advertising.

For eligible adults, Google's User Messaging Platform checks which privacy messages and choices are required. The app waits for this check and Google's permission to request ads before it calls MobileAds.initialize or requests advertising. If a required form is open, the app does not request or show another ad. If the service is unavailable, gameplay remains available.

Information Google's services may process

The customer app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on your choices, device and region, these services may process IP addresses (which can indicate general location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, performance information and diagnostics. Google uses information for advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention and privacy choices. EPATA LLC does not ask the app for your precise device location, contacts, photos, microphone or a birthday for advertising.

Google's processing is described in its Privacy Policy and advertising information. EPATA LLC may receive aggregate advertising reports from its advertising provider. Development and owner-test builds use test advertising and do not establish real customer impressions or revenue.

Where ads appear and how rewards work

Banners may appear in menus in a separate area from the game. An occasional interstitial may appear between completed voyages, with a minimum three-minute interval. Videos for 30 cosmetic shells are voluntary. Only the advertising SDK's completed-reward callback creates a reward receipt. The app stores that receipt and its acknowledgement locally so reloading does not duplicate a reward. Watching ads is never required for retries or game progression.

Version 0.4.1 contains a monthly purchase and restoration interface, with checkout disabled until its Google Play catalog and verification service are accepted. All adventures currently remain available. Local cosmetic receipts do not represent a paid purchase or a restorable store entitlement.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are currently disabled in the version being prepared for Google Play. No purchase-verification account service is enabled for this version. Playing does not require a Google sign-in, and all adventures remain available.

The planned Waddlebound Full subscription is for this app only, with all features and no ads or optional videos. No new lifetime buyout or permanent feature pack is planned. Genuine existing permanent ownership must remain restorable. Before purchases are enabled, we will update this policy with the verification service's data collection, retention and deletion practices. Google Play will handle payment and subscription management; the app will not receive payment-card details. The exact localized recurring price will be shown before checkout.

Deleting purchase-account data

There is currently no active Waddlebound purchase-account service from which to delete records. For privacy questions or a request concerning any purchase data previously supplied to EPATA LLC, contact android-support@epatallc.com. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, Google ID tokens or purchase tokens. We may need to authenticate ownership before acting on a request.

Removing local game progress is separate from cancelling a Google Play subscription or requesting a refund. See the account-data deletion instructions.

Changing your choices

Open Settings → Advertising settings to change the age-group setting. Choosing “Under 18 or unsure” turns advertising off. Settings → Privacy choices opens Google's privacy form when Google requires that option. This is separate from the device's own advertising-identifier controls and any rights available through Google.

Age selection does not delete Google's previously stored privacy choices. Use Google's privacy form when it is available to review those choices. Required consent is checked again on subsequent app launches for eligible adults.

Retention and removal

Game saves, the age-group setting and cosmetic receipts remain on your device until you remove them through Android's app-data controls or uninstall the app. Android app backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app. EPATA LLC cannot recover a local save after it is deleted and does not have a server copy to erase. Google's retention and privacy controls are governed by Google's policies.

External websites and donations

Conservation and donation links open in your browser only after you choose them. Those independent organizations have their own privacy and donation terms. Waddlebound does not collect donations or receive a commission. Playing or watching an ad does not make a donation and does not imply an endorsement by a conservation organization.

Contact and updates

For questions about Waddlebound or this policy, contact EPATA LLC at android-support@epatallc.com. We will update this page when the app's data practices change. Its date appears above.
