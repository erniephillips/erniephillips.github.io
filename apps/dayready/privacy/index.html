DayReady privacy notice

Last updated: November 18, 2026. Covers the regular DayReady Android app, version 0.1.6.

Who provides DayReady

EPATA LLC provides DayReady. For privacy or support requests, contact android-support@epatallc.com. This notice describes the app's current behavior; installing it does not create a DayReady account.

Your plans stay on your device

Instructions you type or share into DayReady, titles, dates, event time zones, steps, materials, availability windows, buffer and travel estimates, templates and progress are stored in the app's private workspace. Core planning works offline without signing in. DayReady does not send those contents to our servers, Google ads, a purchase verifier or an AI service. It does not read your calendar, contacts, inbox or photos. Travel is your estimate; DayReady does not request routes, traffic or device location. There is no automatic document recognition or cloud extraction.

DayReady requests Android notification permission if you enable reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally; Android can delay them. Optional read-aloud selects an installed voice that does not require a network connection. If an appropriate offline voice is unavailable, the written steps still work.

Exports and deletion of local plans

You choose whether to export or import using Android's file picker. An export includes the plan information you chose to save and may contain private instructions. Its destination can be a local folder or a third-party cloud/file service you choose; that service has its own privacy practices. DayReady does not upload exports automatically. Android backup and device transfer of the private workspace are disabled. Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall. Uninstalling can permanently remove plans that you have not exported. Copies you saved elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Advertising and consent

The regular free app integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and Google User Messaging Platform 4.0.0. Google may receive an IP address and approximate location derived from it, advertising/app/device or applicable account identifiers, app/ad interactions and performance or diagnostic information. Google processes this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention under its policies. Ad requests do not contain your plan contents. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean the SDK collects no device data.

Before production ad requests, DayReady checks Google's current consent permission. A required consent form is offered on a suitable app screen. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Ad privacy choices appears in Settings and Plus so you can review or change choices. Ads pause if the required permission is unavailable. You can also use Android's advertising-ID controls. Google controls its own data retention and account/privacy tools. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

Banners are limited to the overview. An occasional capped full-screen ad can appear after an explicit return from a completed plan. Rewarded videos are optional: only Google's earned-reward callback starts a temporary ad break. Being offline, closing an ad or having no available ad never prevents core planning. Development/owner test installers use Google demo inventory; their test activity does not create publisher ad revenue.

Purchases and verified benefits

New real purchases are disabled in version 0.1.6: the store catalog, Google sign-in and verification service are not configured for this release candidate. Purchase and restore controls explain that limitation; they do not charge or grant unverified benefits. DayReady keeps the native verification rules for existing ad-free/full-access benefits. If a configured purchase-verification service is introduced in a later release, Google handles payment and sign-in. That service uses purchase tokens, product and transaction identifiers, an account reference and an app-installation public-key proof to verify and restore ownership. It does not receive your plans or payment-card details. The service design encrypts purchase tokens and hashes the account reference; signed access is bound to the installation. A local verified lease lasts up to 24 hours and never beyond the paid subscription period.

Where available, Sign out removes cached paid access from this device while keeping plans. Delete paid-access account data requests deletion of verification records; it does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase, remove Google's records or delete local plans. Restoring purchases can create new verification records. Manage any subscription through Google Play. Provider operations and payment records are subject to their own retention and legal requirements.

Deleting information and requesting account-data deletion

To delete a local plan, open that plan, choose Delete plan and confirm. To remove the complete local workspace, use Android Settings, Apps, DayReady, Storage, Clear data, or uninstall; export anything you need first. Delete exported files separately at their selected destinations. These are user-controlled deletion choices, not steps required for an update.

This candidate does not create an EPATA paid-access account because its verification service is not configured. When the app has a verified signed-in account, Plus offers Delete paid-access account data with confirmation and Sign out of paid access. For an external request about information you supplied to EPATA LLC, email android-support@epatallc.com with the subject DayReady data deletion and identify the account or prior support contact. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private plans. We will confirm the applicable records, identity verification and any legally required retention before reporting deletion. A request to EPATA does not cancel a Google Play subscription or delete data held independently by Google or an export provider. No automatic deletion deadline is promised.

Your choices and requests

You can use free planning without an account, leave optional notifications and read-aloud off, manage ad choices where provided, export plans and delete local information. This app does not include a separate analytics, cloud AI or crash-reporting service; Google's ad SDK may still process its own diagnostics. If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send. Please omit private instructions or other people's information unless necessary to explain the issue. Contact the address above for privacy questions or requests concerning information you supplied to EPATA LLC.

Device and family use

DayReady helps a person manage their own activities and preparations. Putting a school or family activity in a local plan does not create an account for another person or send that plan to advertisers. The person managing the device should choose appropriate content and permissions. Store audience declarations and any child-related advertising requirements must match the app's actual distribution and use.

Security and changes

The workspace uses Android app-private storage. Configured verification connections use HTTPS, and Google describes its SDK transport as TLS. No system can guarantee protection against every device compromise. We will revise this notice when the app's data behavior changes; the updated notice will identify its revision date.
