Request deletion of UploadReady account data

The free file tools do not require an account. Optional Google sign-in supports purchase verification when that service is enabled.

From the app

  1. Connect to the internet and open UploadReady.
  2. Open Ad-free version and sign in with the Google account used for verification, if necessary.
  3. In UploadReady Plus, choose Delete Plus account data, then Delete account data.
  4. Wait for the confirmation that stored account data was deleted. An error or sign-out alone does not confirm server deletion.

Without the app

Email android@epatallc.com with the subject UploadReady account data deletion. Say that you want the UploadReady verification account and associated purchase-verification records deleted. We may need to verify account ownership before completing the request. Do not send private documents, passwords, payment-card details or purchase tokens.

What deletion covers

A successful authenticated deletion removes the service's purchase-verification records for the account and clears the app's saved sign-in and access records. Restoring a purchase may create verification records again. Google maintains its own account and transaction records under its policies.

Deletion does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase or subscription. Manage subscriptions in Google Play. It does not erase your local documents, original files or copies saved or shared elsewhere.

Remove local documents

Delete individual prepared files in My files, or use Privacy and storage → Delete all app copies to remove the local workspace, exports and custom presets. External saved/shared copies remain in the location or app you chose.
