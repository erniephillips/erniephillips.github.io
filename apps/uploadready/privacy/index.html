UploadReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

UploadReady prepares photos and PDF pages for file uploads. This notice explains how the UploadReady Android app, provided by EPATA LLC, handles your information.

Your documents stay on your device

File preparation works locally. UploadReady does not send document contents, filenames, thumbnails, previews or file checksums to EPATA, Google ads, Google sign-in or the purchase-verification service. An account is not required for the free file tools.

The app imports copies of files you select or photos you choose to capture. Your source photos and PDFs are preserved. Imported copies, prepared exports, saved presets and file history stay in the app's local storage until you delete them. Previews are generated on your device. Renaming a prepared file changes its Save and Share display name, not its contents or copies already saved elsewhere. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Saving or sharing creates a copy in the location or app you choose. Selecting a cloud file provider or sharing app can involve that provider's services and privacy terms. Deleting UploadReady's copies does not delete your originals or copies saved or shared elsewhere.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage applicable consent. Google's SDKs can process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived general location, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Ad requests do not include your document data. UploadReady requests non-personalized ads; that setting does not mean the SDK receives no information.

Where Google requires privacy choices, you can open Ad privacy choices from Privacy and storage or the UploadReady Plus screen. The app follows the SDK's current permission before requesting production ads. Device-level advertising privacy settings are also available in Android.

A verified ad-free purchase or active Monthly Full Access removes banners, interstitials and video offers. An optional completed rewarded video can pause ads for 30 minutes in the current session. A video or purchase is never required to use the free file tools.

Learn more about Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information for advertising. Development builds use Google test ads, which do not generate advertising revenue.

Optional purchases and Google sign-in

Google Play handles payments when purchases are enabled. EPATA does not receive your payment-card number. Google sign-in is used only when you choose to buy, restore or manage verification-account data; it is not required to prepare files.

When the purchase-verification service is enabled, it receives a Google identity token, an installation public key and identifier, product identifiers, purchase tokens and signed verification requests. A Google identity token can include standard account claims such as your email address. The service uses the account identifier to confirm purchases with Google and issue installation-bound access records. The implemented service does not store your Google display name, email address or avatar. It stores purchase-verification records associated with a pseudonymous account identifier; purchase tokens are encrypted at rest. These records do not contain your documents.

Verified access records are also kept on your device. A signed offline access record lasts at most 24 hours between verification checks; monthly benefits also stop at the verified subscription expiry if sooner. This also means a refund or revocation may not take effect on an offline device until its existing record expires. One-time purchases are rechecked separately and do not become subscriptions. The free file tools remain usable without paid verification. Development and store-draft test builds have real purchases disabled and cannot charge you.

Deletion and retention

You can remove individual prepared files in My files. Privacy and storage → Delete all app copies removes the local workspace, exports and custom presets. Uninstalling removes app-private data; files you saved outside the app remain in their chosen locations.

If you use the verification service, its purchase records remain until removed through the authenticated account-data deletion feature. When signed in and online, open Ad-free version → UploadReady Plus → Delete Plus account data, then confirm Delete account data. A successful request deletes the service's stored purchase records for your account, removes the app's saved sign-in and access records, and signs you out. It does not delete local documents. Signing out alone removes local sign-in and access records but does not delete the service's purchase records. Restoring a purchase can create verification records again.

You can also contact android@epatallc.com for help requesting deletion; do not send a password, document or purchase token. Account ownership may need to be verified before a request can be completed. Google retains its own transaction and account records under its policies. Signing out or deleting verification-account data does not cancel a subscription or refund a Google Play purchase; manage cancellation in Google Play.

Contact and updates

For UploadReady privacy questions or help with a deletion request, contact EPATA LLC at android@epatallc.com. Do not email private documents, passwords or purchase tokens.

We will update this notice when the app's data handling changes and revise the date above. This policy applies to UploadReady; other EPATA apps may have different data practices.
