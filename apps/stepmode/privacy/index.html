StepMode Privacy Policy

This policy describes the regular StepMode Android app, package com.epata.stepmode, provided by EPATA LLC.

Your documents and offline AI

StepMode processes the pages, photographs, files, and text you choose on your device. Text recognition and the included AI model run locally. The document workspace needs no account, cloud AI service, API key, or separate model download.

StepMode does not automatically upload document text, images, filenames, prompts, findings, source quotations, document hashes, or your review notes to EPATA, advertising providers, or the purchase service. It does not use your documents to train a remote AI model.

StepMode creates temporary app-private processing files. It removes these when you end the session and cleans interrupted temporary sessions at startup. It does not create document history automatically. If you choose Save, the saved document is encrypted with a key protected by Android Keystore and retained on your device until you delete it. Saved private documents are excluded from the app's Android backup and device-transfer configuration. Importing a file does not delete your original.

Advertising and privacy choices

The regular app includes Google AdMob advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform for ad privacy choices. Ads appear on App home, after private document processing has ended. They do not appear during document input, editing, review, AI processing, checklists, or Help. Rewarded video is optional; an earned reward provides 12 hours without ads. Verified paid ad-free access removes advertisements and video offers.

When these Google services are contacted, Google may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, app or account identifiers permitted by Android, ad interactions such as views and taps, and performance or diagnostic information. These services use data for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. StepMode requests non-personalized ads and removes the Android advertising-ID permission; this does not mean that ad services collect no data.

Where applicable, StepMode presents Google's privacy message before requesting ads. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Ad privacy options is available on App home, including during an earned ad break or paid access. You can use the offline document tools if ads or consent services are unavailable. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure.

Optional purchases and accounts

Where paid checkout and restore are available, they use Google Play and optional Google sign-in. EPATA's verification service receives a Google sign-in token to verify your identity, and purchase tokens and product details to verify ownership, paid-through dates, refunds, and revocations. This service receives network connection information such as your IP address. Document content is not included in these requests.

The verification service stores a derived account identifier, purchase records and tokens, and short-lived authentication and request records. It does not store raw Google sign-in tokens or raw Google account subjects. The device stores a signed access record so verified permanent purchases and the verified paid subscription period can work offline. EPATA does not receive your payment-card details from Google Play.

Signing out clears the purchase session and access cache on that device. Delete purchase account records, when an account is available, deletes the verification service's purchase records and sessions and clears the device's access cache. It does not delete your local documents, erase Google's payment records, or cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. A later explicit restore can recreate verification records for purchases you still own. Purchase verification records are retained until you request deletion; temporary authentication and request records expire according to their purpose.

Export, sharing, and Android services

Export and sharing happen when you request them. Files you export and the app or person you share with may retain that information under their own settings and policies. Deleting a StepMode save does not delete those external copies or your original imported file.

Your chosen keyboard, camera app, text-to-speech engine, accessibility services, Android, and other apps have their own privacy behavior. StepMode cannot control data those services process. Use a device speech engine that supports offline operation if you want offline reading aloud.

Retention, security, and your choices

You can delete saved documents in StepMode. Temporary processing data is cleaned as described above. Local ad choices, earned ad-break timing, and non-content session counts remain on the device as needed for their function. Removing the app removes its private local storage; copies outside the app and Google's records are separate.

Saved-document encryption and restrictions on document-workspace networking help protect private work. Advertising and purchase verification use encrypted network connections. No device, software, or network connection can guarantee protection against every compromise. EPATA does not sell your private document content or supply it to advertising providers.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or help requesting purchase-account deletion, email epata.llc.co@gmail.com. Do not attach private documents or payment credentials unless they are necessary for your request and you choose to share them.

EPATA may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the current text. Changes that require a new privacy choice will be presented through the app's applicable privacy flow.
