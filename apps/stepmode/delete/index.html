Delete StepMode account records and local data

The document tools in StepMode, package com.epata.stepmode, need no account. A purchase account is used only where optional paid checkout and restore are available.

Delete a purchase account

When the purchase service is available, open App home, expand Optional upgrades & ad support, and sign in to the purchase account if needed. Choose Delete purchase account records, read the confirmation, and choose Delete records.

This removes the verification service’s stored purchase records, purchase tokens, authentication sessions and request records for that account, and clears the device’s access cache. It does not erase Google’s payment records, delete your local documents or cancel a subscription in Google Play. Cancel renewal in Google Play separately. An explicit same-owner restore can recreate verification records for purchases still owned.

If you cannot use the app, request deletion help at epata.llc.co@gmail.com. Identify StepMode and the purchase account email. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens, payment-card details or private documents. Account ownership must be verified before records are removed.

Delete documents from this device

  • End private session removes temporary processing copies. Startup also cleans interrupted temporary work.
  • Open Privacy & local AI, find a saved document, use its Delete control and confirm. This deletes the encrypted saved copy. An open session remains until you end it.
  • Exports, shared copies and original files are separate. Delete them using the destination app or file manager if you want to remove them.

Removing StepMode removes access to its app-private local storage. StepMode does not automatically retain document history or send your documents to the purchase service.
