Noctiloom Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy describes the regular Android edition of Noctiloom, a puzzle game from EPATA LLC. Core puzzles work offline and do not require an account. Google advertising and store integrations can use online services as described below.

Your game on your device

Puzzle progress, unfinished campaign games, earned motes, owned colors, tutorial status and sound/motion preferences are stored in the app's private device storage. The game does not upload its puzzle-history save file to EPATA or add a separate EPATA gameplay-analytics service. Google advertising services can still process the activity and diagnostic information described below.

The app is configured to exclude its private files and settings from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can erase your local progress. Noctiloom does not request microphone, camera, contacts or precise-location permission. Gameplay does not use a cloud AI service. The tutorial movie, artwork and sounds are included in the installation.

Advertising and Google services

The regular edition includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. These services can process IP address, approximate location derived from it, device/advertising identifiers, applicable account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.

The app requests nonpersonalized ads. Those requests can still involve data processing for ad delivery and fraud prevention. Ad requests use Google's consent-permission decision and wait for ad SDK initialization. Required privacy forms are presented from Home. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Ad privacy choices appears in Settings and Privacy & credits. If privacy setup fails, a Retry ad privacy setup option can appear instead.

A Home banner and occasional ads between eligible completed campaign puzzles help support the game. Rewarded videos are optional; completing an eligible video grants in-game motes with no cash value. An unavailable ad or an uncompleted optional video does not block core puzzles.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Google's retention practices and privacy controls apply to information handled by its services. Android's settings also provide advertising-identifier controls.

Current store status and conditional account features

Store-account and purchase-verification features are inactive in the current development configuration. The Android integration can still connect to Google Play and request product-availability information even while buying is unavailable. Requesting that information does not complete a purchase or create an EPATA store account.

The Android integration can also create and keep a local installation identifier and key in private Android storage for store verification. Creating that key does not by itself send it to EPATA. Signing in to an EPATA store account, verifying purchases, restoring benefits, redeeming codes and deleting that account require an activated verification service.

If those features are activated in a configured release, purchases and subscriptions would use Google Play. Optional Google sign-in would supply a sign-in token. EPATA's verification service would process that token, an account identifier, an installation identifier/public key, purchase tokens and payment/product status to verify, restore and manage benefits and prevent duplicate grants. Payment-card details are handled through Google Play and are not entered into Noctiloom.

This policy will be updated with the deployed service's retention and account-deletion arrangements before those features are activated. No operational EPATA store-account deletion service is offered in the current development configuration. Removing local game data or uninstalling does not cancel any Google Play subscription; subscriptions are managed through Google Play's subscription controls.

Your choices and requests

You can play core puzzles offline, decline an optional video, use available ad privacy choices and change local preferences. Android's app-storage controls can erase local progress. Contact EPATA with privacy questions or requests about information it handles using the address below. Requests concerning Google's separate services can also be directed through Google's privacy controls. Applicable rights depend on where you live.

Children and age information

Noctiloom's own interface does not ask for your age or date of birth. Google services have their own age and privacy requirements. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about information relating to a child, contact EPATA using the address below; Google's separate privacy controls also apply to its services.

Contact and changes

Contact EPATA LLC at android@epatallc.com with Noctiloom privacy questions. This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change; the date above identifies the version.
