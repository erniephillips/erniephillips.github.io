EPATA Puzzle Arcade privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Android application: com.epata.glow

This policy explains information handled by the regular EPATA Puzzle Arcade Android app, its advertising services, optional paid-access verification, and EPATA LLC support.

  • Your puzzle boards, completed games, and settings stay on your device.
  • All ages may see banner ads. Children, teens, and skipped ages use child-appropriate ad settings, without video or full-screen advertisements.
  • An optional birthday form runs on this phone. Only an age group is saved; the birthday is discarded.
  • Optional purchases use Google Play and verified account access. Free games do not require sign-in.
  • Use Settings for local-data controls and available ad privacy choices. Contact android-support@epatallc.com for privacy requests.

Games and information stored on your device

EPATA Puzzle Arcade stores your current puzzle, completed-game records, elapsed play times, level progress, tutorial completion, theme, and sound preferences in this app’s local storage. These records support saving and resuming play. We do not upload your game history or provide cloud progress synchronization. Android automatic backup is disabled for this app.

The app does not request access to your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location. An optional, neutral birthday form chooses age-appropriate settings locally. It saves only a group: child, teen, adult, or unknown. It does not store or upload the birthday. All six games remain available when you skip this form. Sharing a challenge uses the Android share chooser; a grown-up check comes first in protected play, and you choose the destination and information sent.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free app uses Google Mobile Ads for banner advertisements. Adult age settings can additionally enable voluntary hint videos and occasional advertisements between completed puzzles. A video is optional; a hint is awarded only after Google reports that the video reward was earned. Verified full-access purchases remove these advertisements and provide their hint benefits directly. Children, teens, and unknown ages use protected play: child-directed, nonpersonalized banner ads with a maximum ad content rating of G. Reward videos and full-screen advertisements are not loaded or shown in that mode. Standard free hints and guided lessons remain available. Skipping age setup does not remove banner ads.

Google’s advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, advertising IDs, app-set IDs, and applicable device or account identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Requesting nonpersonalized ads does not make all of this processing anonymous. EPATA Puzzle Arcade requests nonpersonalized ads and a maximum ad content rating of G.

Google’s User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent prompts. Production ad requests wait until that service permits them. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, Settings displays Ad privacy choices so you can review or change the available choices. You can also use your device’s Android advertising-ID controls. We do not operate a separate first-party behavioral analytics service.

For Google’s practices, see Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information, and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Optional sign-in, purchases, and restoration

When paid access is available and an adult chooses to purchase or restore it, Google sign-in and Google Play handle identity and payment. EPATA LLC does not receive your card number. Our verification service checks your Google identity and Play purchase before granting paid benefits. Protected play does not start the online billing connection, Google sign-in, or server purchase-verification requests. An existing, valid signed access proof may continue to provide previously purchased benefits until it expires.

Verification uses a hashed Google account identifier, the app and product identifiers, a purchase token, purchase-status and verification times, and your installation’s public key or fingerprint. Purchase tokens in our verification database are encrypted. Raw Google identity tokens and Google profile fields are not stored in that database. The installation’s private signing key stays in Android Keystore on your device.

The app keeps signed access proofs in its private storage. Existing verified access can work offline for up to 24 hours between checks, subject to a subscription’s paid-through date. Google and our verification service are contacted when sign-in, purchase verification, restoration, or an access refresh requires them. Your local game history is not part of these verification requests.

Retention, deletion, and your controls

Local progress and settings remain until you reset them in Settings or remove this app’s Android data. Removing the app also removes its local app data. These actions do not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase or subscription.

Account-linked purchase-verification records are retained to verify and restore paid access. After signing in to Plus, Settings provides Delete Plus account data to delete our stored purchase links and clear local access credentials. Sign out of Plus clears the local sign-in and access state. Deletion does not remove records independently held by Google, and later restoration can create a new verified purchase link. Manage subscription renewal and refunds through Google Play.

You may also request deletion or ask about your information by emailing android-support@epatallc.com with “EPATA Puzzle Arcade privacy request” in the subject. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account. Do not include passwords or payment-card numbers. Support receives the email address and message you send and uses them to handle your request.

Family use

This app is intended for all ages. Children, teens, and skipped ages receive protected play. Google ad requests use child age treatment, a maximum content rating of G, and nonpersonalized-ad settings. The consent SDK is tagged as under age of consent; no personalized-ad consent is requested for that group. Only banner ads are used in protected play. Online billing and purchase sign-in services do not start for that group. Age settings are separate from game saves and cannot grant paid features. The optional birthday is processed locally and discarded. The saved group does not identify a person.

Use Settings → Age and privacy settings to review the local age setup. Switching from adult settings to protected play requires closing and reopening the app so advertising SDKs restart using the more restrictive age settings. Local game saves are preserved. Parents or guardians can also clear Android app data or contact EPATA LLC about a child’s information.

Service providers and security

Google provides advertising, consent, sign-in, and Play billing services. Our purchase-verification service handles only the information needed for verified access. Connections to that service use HTTPS. Local access credentials are kept outside the game’s JavaScript storage, and private server credentials are not included in the app.

We will update this policy when the app’s information practices change. The date at the top identifies this version.
